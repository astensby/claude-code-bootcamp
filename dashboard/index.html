Assumptions

  1. All timestamps are UTC. Days and weekdays are cut at UTC midnight, not local time.
  2. About 2% of ts values ( rows) use DD/MM/YYYY HH:mm instead of ISO 8601. They were parsed as day-first UTC and kept, not dropped.
  3. newsletter and direct are not hostnames: they mean tracked mail and unknown source. They are shown as referrers anyway.
  4. A click is a row in clicks.csv. No bot filtering, no de-duplication.
  5. "Top links" counts every click in the window regardless of when the link was created, so an old link can outrank a new one that is doing better per day.
  6. The window is twelve full weeks, Monday to Sunday, so every weekday appears exactly twelve times in the weekday chart.
  7. Numbers are pre-computed by dashboard/build.mjs from data/; rebuild after npm run gen-data.
